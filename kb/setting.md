# Stratocracy — Setting and Voice Guide

Stratocracy is art-light and systems-forward. This file is the *thin* fiction that
gives generated content a consistent voice — enough for codex flavor and post-match
lines, never so much that it contradicts the rules. The pipeline retrieves the tone
bible for every generation; it retrieves a faction block only for faction-flavored
content (result-screen text).

## Premise

A fractured stratocracy — a state ruled by its military — has split. Two commands
contest the same ground, each trying to destroy the other's **flag unit** (its command
element) or out-hold it to the turn cap. There are no civilians in frame, no lore epics:
just terrain, hardware, and the ledger of Fame.

## Tone bible (applies to ALL generated content)

- **Register:** terse tactical briefing. Field-manual plain, not marketing copy.
- **Person/tense:** present tense; direct. Imperatives are fine ("Screen your armor").
- **Substance over drama:** hardware, terrain, and objectives — not heroes, destiny, or glory.
- **Length:** a codex blurb is <= 40 words. A result line is <= 30 words.
- **Hard rule:** never contradict `rules.md`. No invented units, abilities, costs, or outcomes.
- **Banned register:** avoid melodrama and fantasy filler — words like *destiny, glory,
  honor, legend, forever, epic, heroic, sacred, doom*.

Unit and terrain codex entries use this **neutral field-manual voice** (no faction).
Faction voice appears only in result-screen text.

## Faction A — The Directorate

The incumbent regime that still holds the capital. Voice: cold, doctrinal, bureaucratic-
military. Speaks in directives and ledgers. Values order and continuity; frames every
outcome as a matter of record. Sample cadence: "Command directive:", "Order is restored.",
"The Directorate endures."

## Faction B — The Vanguard

A coalition of breakaway commanders. Voice: terse, pragmatic, defiant. Speaks from the
mud, distrusts ceremony, measures everything by ground held. Sample cadence: "We hold the
ridge.", "Didn't ask permission.", "We scatter, we regroup, we come back."
